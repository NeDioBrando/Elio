## ✨ PlazCode 1.24.20

Fixes Start Agent on ClickUp's Ask Brain, which stopped right after the AI's first reply and never finished connecting.

### 🛠️ What's fixed

- Start Agent now finishes connecting in a new ClickUp Ask Brain chat. Before, it stopped a second after Brain's first reply and said "Not connected to this chat yet".
- ClickUp changes the page address when a new chat gets its first message, and PlazCode mistook that for a different chat. It now knows it is the same chat.
- Nothing else changes: other AI sites, ClickUp agent chats, and your settings, memory and connections stay as they were.

### 🧪 What was checked

- Tried on a real ClickUp Ask Brain chat: all five setup steps went through and PlazCode said it was ready. The same test failed before the fix.
- ClickUp agent chats and Firefox were not changed and not tried on a live account.
- The Windows app is unsigned and the Mac app is not notarized, as before. Your computer may ask you to confirm opening it.

### 📥 How to update

- Open PlazCode, or choose **Updates → Update now**.
- Manual downloads: **PlazCode-1.24.20.zip** for Windows, **PlazCode-macOS-1.24.20.zip** for Mac, or **PlazCode-Firefox-1.24.20.zip** for Firefox.
- Reload your extension and refresh your AI tabs. Firefox users: reload the temporary add-on after restarting Firefox.
- Your settings, memory and connections are kept.

Test details are included in VALIDATION.txt.
